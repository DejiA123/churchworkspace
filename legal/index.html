Church Work Space

Schedule and auto-publish your church's own sermon videos and photos — to TikTok and more, from one desktop app.

What it does

  • Prepare posts once and have them publish by themselves at their scheduled time.
  • Post your own recorded service and sermon videos directly to your TikTok profile.
  • Also supports Facebook Pages, Instagram, and YouTube from the same place.
  • Runs entirely on your own computer and talks directly to each platform's official API.

How TikTok is used

  • Login Kit — you sign in with TikTok once to connect your account.
  • Content Posting API (Direct Post) — the app posts the videos you schedule straight to your own profile.
