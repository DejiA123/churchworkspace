Privacy Policy

Church Work Space · Last updated: July 21, 2026

Church Work Space ("the app") is a desktop application operated by [YOUR CHURCH NAME] to schedule and publish our own media content to our own social media accounts, including TikTok. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the person on our media team who installs and uses the app on their own computer and connects our organization's social media accounts to it.

2. What the app accesses

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok asks you to sign in and approve access. With your approval the app receives, through TikTok's official API:

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): your TikTok open id, display name and avatar. This is used only to show which account is connected inside the app.
  • Content posting access (video.publish): permission to upload and post videos that you choose and schedule in the app to your own TikTok profile.

3. Where your data is stored

The app runs entirely on your own computer. Your TikTok access tokens and connection details are stored locally on that computer only. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes your TikTok data. The app communicates directly with TikTok's API; your videos and tokens are not routed through any third party.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not collect analytics about your TikTok account.
  • We do not access any content on your TikTok account other than to post the videos you schedule.

5. Content you publish

Videos and captions you schedule are your own content and are posted to your own TikTok profile at your direction. You are responsible for the content you choose to publish, in line with TikTok's own Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

6. Removing access

You can disconnect the account at any time inside the app, and you can revoke the app's access from your TikTok account under Settings → Security & permissions → Apps. Removing the app from your computer deletes the locally stored tokens.

7. Children's privacy

The app is a tool for our media team and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
